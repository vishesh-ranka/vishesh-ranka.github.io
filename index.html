02 Projects

PROJECT 01 / FORECASTING

Hierarchical Demand Forecasting & Reconciliation

DuckDB (SQL) Python LightGBM Pandas Nixtla hierarchicalforecast

Built an end-to-end forecasting pipeline for 30,490 product store combinations from a 58M row Walmart (M5) dataset, staging raw data in SQL and generating LightGBM demand forecasts across 5 business levels: company, category, department, store, and item.

Diagnosed a crossed (non-nested) hierarchy structure, where product and geography rollups intersect rather than nest. That structure is the reason a naive bottom-up rollup doesn't reconcile cleanly at every level.

12.5% Improvement in company-wide forecast accuracy
0% Mismatch across all hierarchy levels (exact coherence) vs. up to 2.5% unreconciled
30,490 Product store combinations forecast
5 Business levels reconciled company → item

Applied MinT reconciliation to enforce forecast consistency across all levels, against up to 2.5% inconsistency in the unreconciled forecasts.

PROJECT 02 / OPTIMIZATION

Multi-Echelon Inventory Optimization Under Demand Uncertainty

Python (PuLP) NumPy Pandas Linear programming Monte Carlo simulation Plotly Streamlit

Built a multi-echelon inventory optimization model (linear programming, PuLP) across a 13-node, 3-region global supply network, allocating safety stock between distribution centers and stores under a 95% service level constraint.

Diagnosed autocorrelated demand causing standard safety stock formulas to understate distribution center variability by up to 10.6× over 30–45 day international lead times, and replaced it with empirical quantile based sizing.

100% Distribution center service level, stockouts eliminated vs. 87.7% naive policy
87.7% Service level under the naive safety stock policy
8.3% Holding cost reduction vs. decentralized baseline (up to)
4 Quarters of out-of-sample, rolling-window Monte Carlo validation

Validated via out-of-sample, rolling-window Monte Carlo simulation across 4 quarters: distribution center stockouts were eliminated (100% service level versus 87.7% under the naive policy), and holding cost fell by up to 8.3% versus a decentralized baseline.
